Who We Are

Our Story

Frostline Wellness is Idaho and Utah's trusted source for premium cold plunge systems. As an authorized Plunge Zero dealer, we help homeowners, athletes, wellness facilities, gyms, and recovery centers bring professional-grade cold therapy into their daily routines.

Based in Mountain Home, Idaho, we're passionate about helping people improve recovery, resilience, energy, and overall well-being through cold water immersion. We partner with Plunge Zero because their systems combine powerful cooling, advanced filtration, automatic ozone sanitation, and simple plug-and-play operation into one streamlined package.

Our focus is simple: provide exceptional products, honest guidance, and local support you can count on.
