Cold Plunge Therapy at Home

Cold water immersion at home gives you on-demand access to one of the most effective recovery and wellness tools available. When you submerge in cold water, your body responds by reducing inflammation, constricting blood vessels, and releasing norepinephrine — a hormone that sharpens focus, elevates mood, and builds stress resilience. The effect compounds over time: regular plungers report faster muscle recovery, better sleep, and sustained daily energy.

The PlungeZero Residential unit is built for exactly this routine. With consistent cooling down to 39°F, advanced ozone filtration that keeps water clean between sessions, and simple plug-and-play setup, you get a professional-grade cold therapy experience in your home — without ice, complicated plumbing, or ongoing hassle.
