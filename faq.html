FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about cold plunge therapy and our products.

General Questions

Cold water immersion therapy involves submerging your body in cold water — typically between 39°F and 55°F — for a short period. It triggers a physiological stress response that, over time, improves circulation, reduces inflammation, accelerates muscle recovery, boosts energy, and builds mental resilience.

Cold therapy is used by athletes, fitness enthusiasts, wellness-focused individuals, and anyone looking to improve recovery and daily energy. Both our residential and commercial units are designed for regular use by healthy adults. If you have a medical condition, consult your doctor before starting.

PlungeZero systems can cool water down to approximately 39°F (4°C), well within the optimal range for cold water immersion therapy. The built-in chiller maintains a consistent temperature so you get the same experience every session.

Most practitioners recommend starting with 1–3 minutes and gradually increasing to 10–15 minutes as your body adapts. Listen to your body, exit immediately if you feel lightheaded, and never plunge alone if you're new to cold therapy.
